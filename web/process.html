Process engineering

From a diamond plate to a working chip

Traveler T1, step by step: hydrogen-terminated diamond transistors with optional NV-qubit windows. Each step shows the cross-section, starting-point parameters with sources, the checks to run and their pass windows, safety, and what to do when it fails. Log your measurements as you go; the run log stays in this browser and exports to CSV.

Parameters are starting points from the literature, not a qualified recipe. Safety notes summarize hazards and never replace your facility's training. Markdown version: docs/process/TRAVELER.md.
