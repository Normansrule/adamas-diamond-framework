## Step 8 · S07 Vacancy anneal and oxygen re-termination

**Label:** reported. Heating makes vacancies mobile; when one meets an implanted nitrogen it forms an NV center. The oxidizing clean then stabilizes the negative charge state that is the qubit.

**Parts and machines:** M22 High-vacuum annealing furnace (800–1200 °C); M10 Wet bench: tri-acid and piranha cleans, HF

**Cross-section (schematic, top first).** Before: Vacancies around each implanted ¹⁵N / ¹²C cap / Electronic-grade diamond plate. After: NV pair in the ¹²C cap / Electronic-grade diamond plate.

**Photo:** No openly licensed photo is logged for this step yet; the diagram is code-drawn and schematic.

**Acceptance criteria (measure on your sample):** NV conversion yield (confocal count ÷ implanted N) ≥ 0.5 % ([pezzagna2010]); Echo T₂ of shallow NV (depth about 10 nm) ≥ 10.0 µs (ADAMAS); NV⁻ fraction under green light ≥ 0.7 ([aslam2013])

**Safety:** Furnace hot-zone burns; vacuum implosion shielding. Hazardous steps need your institution's laser, chemical, cleanroom and high-voltage training before you start; these notes do not replace it.

**Troubleshooting:**

- Low yield, dark surface layer: Residual oxygen etched the surface during anneal → Better vacuum; lower temperature; forming gas
- Short T₂: Surface spins or implant damage → Higher anneal temperature, oxygen annealing, deeper NV
