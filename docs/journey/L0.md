## Step 1 · A1.1 Mount the diamond on the antenna

**Label:** demonstrated. Glue the diamond (or a spot of powder) onto the microstrip or inside the loop; the microwave field must reach it.

**Parts and machines:** M30 Green source: LED or under 1 mW 532 nm laser module; M31 NV-rich diamond (irradiated and annealed HPHT); M32 Longpass filter; M33 Silicon photodiode and transimpedance amplifier; M34 Phase-locked loop (PLL) microwave synthesizer, ADF4351 class; M35 Microstrip or loop antenna; M36 Microcontroller (Raspberry Pi Pico)

**Setup (signal order):** Green source → NV diamond on antenna → Red filter → Photodiode → Microcontroller

**Photo:** No openly licensed photo is logged for this step yet; the diagram is code-drawn and schematic.

**Safety:** Even a 1 mW green laser can damage eyes: never look into the beam; use a phone camera to align. Measure the module's output with a laser power meter (borrow one) before use, behind the shortpass filter, because labels on cheap modules are unreliable and leaked infrared is invisible. The LED version avoids laser hazards. Microwave powers here are milliwatts; keep the antenna enclosed and away from the body anyway. Hazardous steps need your institution's laser, chemical, cleanroom and high-voltage training before you start; these notes do not replace it.

**Time and cost (whole experiment, estimates from the experiment card):** one afternoon; US$150 to 400
