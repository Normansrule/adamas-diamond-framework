# Glossary

Plain-language definitions. Acronyms are written out first, with the short form in parentheses.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD) | Growing a film one atomic layer per chemical cycle. Used for aluminum oxide gate insulators on diamond. |
| Bandgap | The energy an electron needs to break free and conduct. Bigger gap: higher voltage and temperature tolerance, harder doping. |
| Baliga Figure of Merit (BFOM) | Scorecard for power switches: $\varepsilon\mu E_c^3$. |
| Chemical Vapor Deposition (CVD) | Growing a solid from a reactive gas. Diamond grows from methane and hydrogen plasma. |
| Coherence time ($T_2$) | How long a qubit keeps its quantum phase. $T_2^{*}$ is the uncorrected version; $T_1$ is the energy-relaxation time. |
| Complementary Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor (CMOS) | The standard chip technology that pairs n-type and p-type transistors. |
| Critical field | The electric field at which a material breaks down. |
| Dipolar coupling | The magnetic force between two spins. Falls as one over distance cubed. |
| Doping | Adding impurity atoms that donate carriers. **n-type** adds electrons, **p-type** adds holes. |
| Enhancement/Depletion (E/D) logic | Logic built from one transistor polarity, using a normally-on transistor as the load. |
| Entanglement | A shared quantum state of two or more qubits that cannot be described qubit by qubit. |
| Field-Effect Transistor (FET) | A switch where a gate voltage controls current in a channel. |
| Heteroepitaxy | Growing a crystal on a different crystal (diamond on iridium or sapphire). |
| High Pressure, High Temperature (HPHT) | Diamond synthesis that imitates the Earth's mantle. |
| Hole | A missing electron that behaves as a positive carrier. |
| Hyperfine coupling | Interaction between an electron spin and a nuclear spin. |
| Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistor (MOSFET) | A FET whose gate is insulated by an oxide. |
| Mobility | How easily carriers move in an electric field. |
| Nitrogen-Vacancy (NV) center | A nitrogen atom beside a missing carbon atom in diamond. The qubit of this framework. |
| Optically Detected Magnetic Resonance (ODMR) | Finding a spin's resonance frequency by watching its fluorescence dim. |
| Photoelectric Detection of Magnetic Resonance (PDMR) | The same, read as an electric current. |
| Qubit | A two-level quantum system; the quantum version of a bit. |
| Rabi oscillation | The back-and-forth rotation of a qubit under a resonant drive. |
| Radio Frequency (RF) | Signals from kilohertz to gigahertz. |
| Surface transfer doping | Creating holes in hydrogen-terminated diamond by letting a surface layer pull electrons out. |
| Two-Dimensional Hole Gas (2DHG) | A sheet of mobile holes just under the diamond surface. |
| Wafer | The thin crystal disk on which chips are built. |
| Zero-field splitting ($D$) | The 2.87 gigahertz gap between NV spin levels with no magnet applied. |
